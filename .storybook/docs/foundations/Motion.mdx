# Motion

<StatusBadge status="draft" />

Motion explains a change and gets out of the way: fast, interruptible, never a long imposed scroll. The durations are rounded from the prototype; the owner reviews them before they are approved.

<MotionDemo
  durations={["--duration-fast", "--duration-medium", "--duration-slow"]}
  easings={["--ease-out", "--ease-spring"]}
/>

## Rules

- `--duration-fast` for state changes, `--duration-medium` and `--duration-slow` for entrances and panels.
- `--ease-out` by default; `--ease-spring` only for small playful details.
- Direct manipulation (dragging the desk) follows the pointer with no lag; only coarse wheel input gets a short ease.
- Longer timings are functional only, such as the held drag that opens Work.
- Page transitions use cross-document View Transitions as progressive enhancement: browsers without support navigate normally.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, bounces, easing and View Transitions are removed; functional steps stay, shortened.
