## Rules

- `--font-size-body` is the floor for reading text. Smaller sizes are for metadata, captions and labels only.
- Reading text uses `--line-height-body` and a measure of `--measure-prose`; chapter leads may be wider and use `text-wrap: balance`.
- Labels are Geist Mono, uppercase, with letter spacing.
- **Don't** use Caveat outside the letter greeting and the notes on desk objects.
- Weights: Onest 400, 500 and 600; Geist Mono 400 and 500; Caveat 500 and 600. The fonts are self-hosted.
