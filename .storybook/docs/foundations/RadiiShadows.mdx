# Radii & shadows

<StatusBadge status="draft" />

Soft shapes and two elevation levels. The radius scale is derived from the prototype; the owner reviews it before it is approved.

## Rules

- `--radius-pill`: pills, buttons, the dock items and the back control.
- `--radius-round`: discs, such as the back control's chevron.
- `--radius-xs` to `--radius-md`: small surfaces and focus outlines.
- `--radius-lg` and `--radius-xl`: cards and dialogs.
- `--shadow-rest` for resting surfaces; `--shadow-lift` for the dock and objects being dragged.

**Source:** `docs/design/v11/styles.css`.
