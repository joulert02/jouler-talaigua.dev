# Content

<StatusBadge status="approved" />

What the copy may say, how it is translated and what blocks a release. Every page follows these rules before it ships.

## Rules

- **Real content only.** No stock images, no invented facts, roles or metrics.
- **Mark what is unconfirmed.** A fact waiting for the owner carries `TODO(verify)`; `npm run placeholders -- --strict` fails while any marker remains in `src/content/`, and the production publish must pass it.
- **Confident and honest.** The copy says clearly what Jouler did and leaves out what he did not. Limits are agreed in review, never written as disclaimers on the page.
- **Growth, not titles.** Staff and Frontend Platform are a direction, never presented as a title already held.
- **No hero checklist.** The projects and the quality of the site carry the professional message, not a list of stack, availability or time zone.
- **A visual that points at text renders that text legibly.**

## Languages

- English lives at `/` and Spanish under `/es/`. Every page ships in both, and a story closes only with both versions approved.
- Translations live in their own files; views and routes never hard-code copy.

## Third parties

- Third-party brands appear as text unless they are credited.
- Personal data in captures is cropped or blurred.
- Recreated third-party screens make no accessibility claims about the third party.
- Photos of people need their permission before they are published.
